# Research basis and limits

The obligation model, J0-J4 and G0-G2 anchors, agent-specific attribution rules and lightweight mode
are a local synthesis. No cited source validates these scales for LLM agents. Do not present them as
ISO compliance, a certified audit method or a scientific measurement of bureaucracy.

- [OECD: Standard Cost Model material](https://www.oecd.org/ctp/administration/39947998.pdf).
  Borrow: decompose information obligations into activities and estimate frequency, time and cost.
  Adaptation: semantic workflow obligations and scenario-specific executions. Limit: administrative
  cost measurement does not establish benefit or capture all interaction effects.
- [ISO: The process approach in ISO 9001:2015](https://www.iso.org/iso/iso9001_2015_process_approach.pdf).
  Borrow: linked activities, inputs/outputs, ownership and risk-proportionate process definition.
  Adaptation: assess necessary function coverage without requiring a document per function.
- [HM Treasury: Orange Book](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/orange-book/the-orange-book-management-of-risk-principles-and-concepts).
  Borrow: connect controls and assurance to objectives, risk and proportionate cost; examine gaps and
  unnecessary duplication. Limit: public-sector governance is not an agent workflow prescription.
- [Adler and Borys, 1996](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=979103).
  Borrow: enabling versus coercive formalization. Limit: degree of formalization alone is not value.
- [DeHart-Davis, Green Tape](https://doi.org/10.1093/jopart/mun004).
  Borrow: means-ends relationship, appropriate control, consistent application and understood purpose.
- [van Loon et al., 2016](https://doi.org/10.1111/puar.12569).
  Borrow: distinguish compliance burden from lack of functionality. Limit: validated employee survey
  measures are not automatically validated for model judgments or textual process descriptions.
- [OECD, Fixing Frictions, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1787/5e9bb35c-en).
  Borrow: examine actual journeys, justified friction and different forms of burden.
- [DORA: Streamlining change approval](https://dora.dev/capabilities/streamlining-change-approval/).
  Borrow: risk-sensitive controls integrated into delivery. Limit: findings do not justify removing
  a specific authorization gate or predict a local speedup without task evidence.
- [SPACE, 2021](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/the-space-of-developer-productivity-theres-more-to-it-than-you-think/).
  Borrow: activity and a single metric do not capture productivity.
- [UK Government Analysis Function: MCDA](https://analysisfunction.civilservice.gov.uk/policy-store/an-introductory-guide-to-mcda/).
  Borrow: explicit objectives, alternatives and value judgments; avoid arbitrary score arithmetic.
  Adaptation: use anchored ordinal judgments rather than a weighted overall rating.
- [Cochrane Handbook, chapter 25](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-25).
  Borrow: before/after change can reflect confounding, co-interventions or changed measurement.
  Limit: use this causal caution, not the clinical review protocol, in workflow diagnosis.
- [Zheng et al., 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685).
  Borrow: evaluate model-judge sensitivity to position, verbosity and self-preference.
  Limit: response-ranking results do not quantify bias in this process-audit skill.
