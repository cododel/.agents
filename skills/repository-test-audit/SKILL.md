# Repository Test Audit

Own audit coverage, inventory/reconciliation, verdicts and synthesis as the primary audit workflow.
Load [testing-evidence](../testing-evidence/SKILL.md) as a required supporting dependency for shared
test-value/oracle/boundary and execution criteria; primary selection does not exclude supporting
skills. Do not create a second policy or apply a unit rubric indiscriminately to all layers.
Read project instructions/profile for actual roots, harnesses and service risks.

## Scope and authority

Resolve requested scope and evidence axes first. A single-test review stays bounded; repository/suite
audit requires a reconciled universe. Audit intent permits investigation and requested reports, not
test deletion/rewrites, production edits, installation or shared-data actions. Targeted safe probes
may be useful within actual execution authority; a verdict is never remediation authorization.
Use existing tools; no new runner/framework/memo, mandatory mutation campaign or full-suite count run.

For suite inventory read [inventory.md](references/inventory.md) before collecting/counting. For
semantic group reviews, overlap, source drift or parallel work read
[review-and-resume.md](references/review-and-resume.md). For verdicts/global reporting read
[verdicts-reporting.md](references/verdicts-reporting.md); scoped reviews need no whole-suite ledger.

## Required audit work

1. Verify checkout/status/HEAD and dirty-source identity, instructions, configs/roots and safe
   collection methods. Record provenance/exclusions and unresolved populations. Collection imports
   can run hooks or contact services; static evidence with explicit gaps beats unsafe counting.
2. Reconcile files, definitions/registrations, collected/static cases, alternate/outside-root,
   generated/manual/support and historical material. Keep their units distinct. Every current item
   maps exactly once to a review group/batch; unknown dynamic expansion is not zero.
3. Read actual assertions, setup/helpers/decisive fakes, production subject and independent basis.
   Name realistic failure, oracle, observable result, executed boundary and proof limits. Group only
   genuinely homogeneous cases; retain each identity and individual rationale for exceptions.
4. Map distinct guarantees and verified overlaps across layers. Reason about a realistic surviving
   defect/counterfactual; mark unexecuted reasoning explicitly. Use targeted RED/GREEN/sensitivity
   only when useful and safe, not universally. Similar names/mocks/source tokens do not prove overlap.
5. Give every **reviewed** group one **retain / strengthen / consolidate / retire / investigate**
   verdict, reasons/evidence/confidence, removal risk and a concrete action/prerequisite. Insufficient
   evidence must identify exactly what to inspect/observe next; generic neutral caution is not done.
   Unread items remain unreviewed, not reviewed-investigate to inflate coverage.
6. Deliver a substantive global judgment plus prioritized actions tied to findings: trustworthy
   boundaries, blind spots/false confidence, maintenance burden and measured execution cost. No
   extrapolated quality rates from selected examples or invented runtime/deletion savings.
7. Reconcile current coverage/fingerprints and report precise completion/remaining work. Inventory,
   source review, independent authority, runtime/skip/duration, sensitivity, exhaustive overlap and
   taxonomy normalization are separate axes. 100% source-record coverage is not executed proof.

## Deliverables and completion

Follow project artifact conventions. For an exhaustive audit retain scope/provenance, reconciled
inventory, item→group/batch ledger, semantic verdict/action records, guarantee/overlap map, human
report and resume state. Compact sound homogeneous groups need no per-test essays/forms; every item
must still map to an actually reviewed group. A focused review may fit in a short response.

Full **source review** requires 100% reconciled current group/case coverage, including separately
accounted source-only populations. Sampling/inventory alone is partial. Investigations and runtime/
overlap gaps may remain after source coverage completes: expose exact IDs/evidence/next steps and
the agreed axis-specific completion. Full evidence audit requires its agreed proof axes; unavailable
parts remain blocked/not-run. Mechanical hash/reference QA does not certify semantic conclusions.

Never retire from scores, counts, smells, coverage or no historic failures. Preserve valuable schema,
API, architecture, source-integrity, graph and mocked decision/interaction guarantees. Consolidation
or retirement of a live guarantee needs verified surviving/replacement protection. Retirement of a
legitimately obsolete requirement instead needs evidence of its cancellation and no dependent
consumers or remaining obligations. Actual deletion always needs separate authorization.
