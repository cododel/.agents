# Verdicts And Actionable Reporting

| Verdict | Required basis and next action |
|---|---|
| retain | Meaningful narrow guarantee with suitable oracle/boundary; preserve it and state limits |
| strengthen | Valuable requirement with a concrete assertion/oracle/boundary gap; specify scenario/change, prerequisites and acceptance proof |
| consolidate | Verified equivalence/overlap; name survivor, merged cases and replacement checks preserving unique protection |
| retire | Live guarantee: verified surviving/replacement protection. Obsolete requirement: evidence of cancellation and no dependent consumers/remaining obligations; no artificial replacement. Exact scope/owner, removal risk and separate deletion authorization in either case |
| investigate | Material unresolved contract/helper/dynamics/overlap/proof; exact missing evidence, safe means to obtain it and decision it changes |

One primary verdict per reviewed group, with evidence/confidence, realistic failure, oracle/observable/
boundary, recommendation, removal risk and residual unknowns. Calibrated uncertainty is mandatory;
generic neutral classification is insufficient. Unread items cannot be marked reviewed-investigate.
No required per-assertion form or scoring framework. Proposed changes remain recommendations.

The global report answers what the suite can be trusted to protect, concrete blind spots and false
confidence, verified duplicates and prioritized next work. Link global actions to group findings,
scope/dependencies/acceptance checks. Prioritize risk and evidence, not removal count. Maintenance
coupling/fixture complexity can support a source-based burden diagnosis; durations/flaky rates/
prospective savings require measurements with selection/source/environment provenance.

Examples: reversed A>B versus B<A with identical operands/boundary may consolidate narrowly;
no-network source-order check needs real zero calls plus allowed-input control; valid-only JSON pool
needs missing second path; version literal differs from independent sourcehash integrity. Retain
actual migration graph resolution and unsafe downgrade decision despite structural/mocked nature.
A source-reviewed weakness is not a demonstrated production incident or executed mutant failure.

Report separate denominators/statuses for inventory, source-review coverage, normative authority,
runtime/skip/durations, executed sensitivity, exhaustive overlap, level normalization and recommendation
QA. 100% current source records can be complete while other axes remain investigate/not-run/partial.
Sampling cannot be branded exhaustive. Unknown dynamics/source drift/unread groups remain exact
blocked remainder. Mechanical QA green does not turn source reasoning into execution proof.

Good synthesis is actionable even when partial: "preserve the suite; strengthen named side-effect/
query/timing gaps first, validate opted-in harnesses, consolidate only these verified pairs; wider
retirement is unsupported." Counts of strengthen are not useless-test rates. Historical aggregates
are not current per-item outcomes. Finish with the actual scope-specific completion and next evidence,
not a claim that all uncertainty or runtime risk has disappeared.
